Project name cactus canyon
Year 2026
About Competitive spinning top game. This game was a group project and was built in the first semester. It is inspired by the wild west and its gambling. The game objective is to collect 10 full chips. The 2-4 players can each bet on a pocket where they believe a spinning top will enter by placing quarter chips there. After that, barricades are set up. Strategically placing barricades on your own ramp can help the spinning top move in the desired direction. Placing barricades on opponents' ramps can block thir way. Depending on if a spinnig top landed in their pocket, players loose their placed chips or get them back doubled.
